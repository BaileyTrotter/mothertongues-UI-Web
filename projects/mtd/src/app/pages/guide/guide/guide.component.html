Alphabet

The Dän Kʼí (Northern Tutchone) alphabet was developed with the Yukon Native Language Centre [link to website?]. The first version of the Dän Kʼí alphabet appeared in the 1977 Selkirk Indian Language Noun Dictionary [include link]. Over the last several decades the alphabet has undergone several updates, and different versions of the alphabet have appeared in various Dän Kʼí language resources. In 2021 the Yukon Native Language Centre published revised Dän Kʼí alphabet charts [link]. Through our work with speakers we have learned that the YNLC alphabet does not accurately represent every speakerʼs way of speaking their language. We are exploring options for how to adjust the current alphabet to be more representative. In the meantime, we are using a variation of YNLCʼs current alphabet, which is shared below.

Each ‘letter’ of the alphabet is introduced in the charts below. Sometimes a combination of letters are used to represent one sound in Dän Kʼí (i.e. <tth> or <kw>*).
